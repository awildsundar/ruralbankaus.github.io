### Overview
Rural Banks Australia (RBA) is an online banking platform that offers quality banking services for those living in Rural Australia. Due to branch closures, tardiness, and withdrawals, Australians living in rural areas have little access to high-quality banking, which Rural Banking Australia aims to provide. By allowing users to access banking services online, Rural Australians no longer need to travel great distances for limited support, but rather they can get support on par with their urban counterparts in the comfort of their home.

### Current State
Currently, RBA's website is limited, with only the Home and Contact Us page being avaliable. The CSS styling and HTML structure for the pages has been defined, so adding new pages to the website will be straightforward.

### Future Updates
Once the backend servers for RBA have been developed, the Signup/Login page will be linked to the database via JavaScript as it has more flexibility. Future pages such as:
- Dashboard
- Settings
- Balance
- Transfer
will be added once the relevant backend has been developed.

### Limitations
Due to limited funds, RBA will not allow users to invest in the stock market or savings, or take out loans. Hopefully, with future funding and a greater user-base, these features can be implemented.
